### What is Palvo?

Palvo gives every organization and community its own private space to talk, share files and stay organized, where **only verified members get in** and **the group owns its conversation history**.

- 🏢 **Organizations**: offices, clinics, shops, agencies, schools
- 🏘️ **Communities**: apartment associations, parent groups, clubs, NGOs
- ✅ **Verified members**: admins approve who joins
- 🔒 **Private by default**: staff leave, the chats stay with the organization
- 📱 **Everywhere**: Android, iOS and the web
